Veg / Non-veg selection rule

Base price of your plan includes up to 2 non-veg days in a week. If you select 3 or more non-veg days, extra kitchen charges will apply.

For example, every extra non-veg day (above 2) can add around Rs 50 to your weekly bill. Final amount will always be confirmed by admin / kitchen team on WhatsApp.
