Weekly 1 Meal Plan

One tiffin per day (Monday to Saturday). Ideal if you want a single fixed lunch or dinner time during office or home.

Schedule start rule:
If you subscribe to this plan today (for example on Monday), your tiffin schedule will start from the next working day (Tuesday). The same applies for any day you subscribe.
