Referral

Invite friends, you earn plan-based credit

This referral program applies only to plans (weekly / monthly tiffin plans). It does not apply to single meals.
Important: When someone uses your referral code, the referrer (you) earns the credit — the friend does not get a discount.

Base prices shown here are for plans only. One-off meals are not included in this program.

Your referral code
NMKFRIEND50

When a friend signs up using your code and activates any plan, you earn 6.25% credit. The friend pays the full plan price.

Credit mapping (referrer earns 6.25%):
• Weekly 1 meal (Rs 2,000) → Rs 125 credit
• Weekly 2 meals (Rs 4,000) → Rs 250 credit
• Monthly 1 meal (Rs 8,000) → Rs 500 credit
• Monthly 2 meals (Rs 16,000) → Rs 1,000 credit

The earned credit can be used later toward future plan payments (wallet / invoice adjustment).

How it works
1. Share your referral code with a friend.
2. Your friend signs up and selects any weekly or monthly plan.
3. Your friend enters your referral code at checkout.
4. After the plan is confirmed/activated, the system checks the plan type.
5. Based on the plan, 6.25% credit will be added to your account.

Single meals are excluded. This program applies to plans only.
