Monthly 2 Meals Plan

Two tiffins per day (Lunch + Dinner), 4 weeks (Mon–Sat). Best for full-day coverage.

Rs 16,000 / month

Schedule start rule

If you subscribe to this plan today (for example on Monday), your first tiffin set (lunch + dinner) will start from the next working day (Tuesday) and continue for four weeks (Mon–Sat).

  • 4 weeks × 6 days (approx. 48 meals)
  • Separate time windows for lunch and dinner
  • Menu rotates across weeks with similar dishes

Preferred delivery time

Select your two daily time windows. Final timing will be confirmed on call / WhatsApp.
