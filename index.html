How it works
  1. Choose a plan – weekly or monthly, 1 or 2 meals per day.
  2. Select your time window – for example lunch 11:00–12:00.
  3. Pick dishes from the menu – based on the current week’s rotation.
  4. We deliver – simple ghar-wala khana to your office or home.
