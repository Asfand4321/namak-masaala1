Monthly 1 Meal Plan

One tiffin per day, 4 weeks (Mon–Sat). Ideal for a consistent lunch or dinner for the whole month.

Rs 8,000 / month

Schedule start rule

If you subscribe to this monthly plan today (for example on Monday), your first tiffin will start from the next working day (Tuesday) and continue for four weeks (Mon–Sat).

  • 4 weeks × 6 days (approximately 24 meals)
  • 1 delivery time window per day
  • Menu rotates per week (Week-1, Week-2, etc.)

Preferred delivery time

Select your one daily time window. Final timing will be confirmed on call / WhatsApp.
